01 · The mark

Three rules in a soft-square tile — the middle rule runs off the right edge

The concept is “a page that moves.” Two contained rules read as a document; the middle one breaking the tile's padding reads as speed, and it is the one detail that survives all the way down to 16px. Never redraw the lines — scale the SVG.

Wordmark spec
Instrument Sans 600 · letter-spacing −0.025em · optical size ≥ 15px. Cap height of the wordmark equals 56% of the tile. Gap between tile and wordmark = 27% of tile width.
Clear space
Keep free space equal to half the tile height on all sides. Never place the lockup on a photo, a gradient, or any fill other than --bg, --surface or --ink.
Don't
No outline version, no gradient tile, no drop shadow on the mark, no rotation, no stacking the wordmark under the tile, no stroke weights other than 6.8% of tile size.

04 · Initials avatars

Six fills, all ≥ 4.5:1 against white text

Squircle tiles (radius 27%), Instrument Sans 600, two initials, letter-spacing 0.02em. Assign deterministically: sum the char codes of the user id, modulo 6 — the same person always gets the same colour, and colour is never the only signal (name and email always sit beside it).
